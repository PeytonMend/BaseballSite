Basic Rules

Each team tries to score runs by hitting the ball and running around four bases.

A game has nine innings. Each team gets a turn to bat in every inning.

Major League Baseball

Major League Baseball (MLB) is the highest level of professional baseball.

Some well known teams are the New York Yankees, Boston Red Sox, and Los Angeles Dodgers.
